🌱 Beginner path — zero to professional in 6 steps

1. Forget the scoreboard

Win rate = how often right. Expectancy = average dollars per trade in units of risk. Only the second pays you.

2. Learn the only formula

E = p×W − (1−p)×L. Example: win half the time (p=0.5), wins 1.8R, losses 1.0R → E = +0.40R. You lose as often as you win and still grow.

3. Price your risk first

Stop goes where the idea is wrong (swept level ± buffer). Target goes at next liquidity. If structural risk > 3% of account, skip or wait — never widen the stop.

4. Size like a desk

Risk 2–3% default, 5% only in broad trends. Same size every trade. Our simulation: 3% on edge → 7.6× median; 10% → 1-in-9 halved.

5. Scale out correctly

Half at +1R, stop to breakeven, runner to 2–3R. It raises win rate — but only raises profit if the runner distribution pays. Verify with MAE/MFE.

6. Audit your journal

Pull last 100 trades. Compute net-R mean, profit factor, longest streak, cost drag. Below zero after costs? Fix or drop the setup — never size it up.
